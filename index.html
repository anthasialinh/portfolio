Portfolio Construction & Risk–Return Analysis

An empirical quantitative study evaluating multi-asset portfolio diversification using six major Vietnamese listed equities—MWG, HPG, FPT, TCB, VIC, MBB—benchmarked against the VN-Index.

04 Academic Research
Peer-Reviewed Conference Paper

Integrating Artificial Intelligence into Finance and Accounting Education: Lessons from Global Universities

Author / Researcher: Pham Mai Linh
Conference: ACBSP Region 10 Annual Conference 2026
Scope: 7 International Universities

Research Abstract

The rapid proliferation of generative artificial intelligence, algorithmic automation, and high-frequency analytical workflows is transforming the financial services landscape. This research investigates how business schools globally adapt their curricula to bridge the emerging gap between traditional financial theory and machine-learning-driven practice.

Comparative Framework

By analyzing finance and accounting curricula across seven international universities representing varied institutional traditions (North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific), this study categorizes pedagogical structures across three core pillars:

Pillar 1
Technical Analytics

Embedding Python, algorithmic modeling & NLP in financial reporting courses.

Pillar 2
Governance & Ethics

Model auditing, hallucination risks, and data integrity in accounting compliance.

Pillar 3
Human-in-the-Loop

Synthesizing computational outputs into strategic executive decisions.

Implications for Higher Education in Vietnam

The study outlines practical implementation recommendations for Vietnamese institutions—such as Foreign Trade University (FTU)—to enhance student competitive readiness in banking and corporate FP&A by integrating analytical computational modules without compromising core accounting foundations.
